# Addendum — endstop repair and polarity update

The user reports that the Y-endstop wire has been repaired and that all mechanical endstops are now working. All switches are currently released.

On 2026-09-23, Klipper ran a 30-second `QUERY_ENDSTOPS` poll, issuing 120 queries at 4 queries per second. During the poll, Y changed between `open` and `TRIGGERED` while it was actuated. X, Z, and Z1 remained `TRIGGERED`; the user confirmed they were not touched and were physically released. This showed that the configured logical polarity did not match the expected released state.

The first follow-up reversed `endstop_pin` polarity for X, Z, and Z1 in the repository config and local phase-one working copy. A later X/Y poll showed Y also had reversed logic, so its polarity was changed as well. Final values are:

| Axis/input | Pin | Before | Updated |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| X | PA15 | `!PA15` | `PA15` |
| Y | PA12 | `!PA12` | `PA12` |
| Z | PA11 | `!PA11` | `PA11` |
| Z1 | PC4 | `!PC4` | `PC4` |

### X/Y homing check

In a second 30-second poll, released X reported `open`; pressing it reported `TRIGGERED`. Released Y initially reported `TRIGGERED`, while pressing it reported `open`, so Y polarity was reversed from `!PA12` to `PA12`.

The user confirmed the X endstop is on the right and the Y endstop is at the screen/front. The config uses X+ homing to max=300 and Y− homing to min=0. `G28 X Y` completed successfully. At X=300/Y=0 both endstops reported `TRIGGERED`. After a 50 mm move inward to X=250/Y=50, both returned to `open`. The current Klipper session reports XY homed; Z is not homed.

The final X/Y polarity and homing directions are verified.

### Right/left Z switch poll

A 45-second `QUERY_ENDSTOPS` poll issued 180 queries. All four inputs began `open`. Touching the physical right Z switch changed `stepper_z`/PA11 to `TRIGGERED`; releasing it returned to `open`. Touching the physical left Z switch changed `z1`/PC4 to `TRIGGERED`; releasing it returned to `open`. The final query showed all switches `open`.

This confirms the right Z switch is on PA11 and the left Z switch is on PC4, with released=`open` and pressed=`TRIGGERED`. At the time of this poll, no Z motor movement or Z homing had yet been performed.

### Z direction and homing

The first low-speed `G28 Z` attempt moved the whole portal in the wrong direction. An emergency stop was sent, Klipper shut down, and the command was treated as incomplete. Both Z direction pins were inverted (`PB4` for `stepper_z`, `PA1` for `stepper_z1`), then Klipper was restarted. All endstops read `open` before the retry.

The second `G28 Z` completed at 1 mm/s. The next `QUERY_ENDSTOPS` reported both `stepper_z`/PA11 and `z1`/PC4 as `TRIGGERED`; X and Y were `open`. This verifies Z homing to both physical switches with the updated direction pins. Individual motor-only operation and gantry alignment remain unchecked. At that stage Klipper reported Z homed at 0; the later temperature-sensor restart reset all axes to unhomed.

No heater commands were sent. XY homing and the inward 50 mm move were completed earlier. After adding the read-only bed sensor and restarting Klipper, all axes are unhomed. Prior jog coordinates were estimates, not calibration.

### Read-only bed temperature check

Using Makerbase's Robin Nano V1.2 pinout and upstream Klipper's generic board config, a read-only `[temperature_sensor bed]` was added on PC0/TB with `EPCOS 100K B57560G104F`. It reported 18.39°C, then 18.49°C with the bed unheated. No bed heater output was configured or enabled. The value is plausible at idle but not calibrated, and the stock thermistor type has not been independently confirmed against Creativity's factory firmware. See `docs/STATUS.md` for source links.
