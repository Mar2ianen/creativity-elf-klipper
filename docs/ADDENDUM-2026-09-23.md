# Addendum — endstop repair and polarity update

The user reports that the Y-endstop wire has been repaired and that all mechanical endstops are now working. All switches are currently released.

On 2026-09-23, Klipper ran a 30-second `QUERY_ENDSTOPS` poll, issuing 120 queries at 4 queries per second. During the poll, Y changed between `open` and `TRIGGERED` while it was actuated. X, Z, and Z1 remained `TRIGGERED`; the user confirmed they were not touched and were physically released. This showed that the configured logical polarity did not match the expected released state.

The `endstop_pin` polarity was reversed for X, Z, and Z1 in both `config/printer.cfg` and `outputs/Creativity_Elf_phase1.cfg`. Y toggled during actuation and retains its prior polarity pending a deliberate press/release check:

| Axis/input | Pin | Before | Updated |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| X | PA15 | `!PA15` | `PA15` |
| Y | PA12 | `!PA12` | `!PA12` (retained) |
| Z | PA11 | `!PA11` | `PA11` |
| Z1 | PC4 | `!PC4` | `PC4` |

With the updated polarity, X, Z, and Z1 should report `open` when released and `TRIGGERED` when pressed. This updated behavior has not yet been checked with a new poll. Y toggled during the test, but its released/pressed mapping was not established conclusively. Before homing, verify each switch separately with `QUERY_ENDSTOPS`, including that it returns to `open` after release. The Z1-to-PC4 assignment remains provisional and needs physical confirmation.

No motion or heater commands were sent during the poll. The printer remains unhomed; prior jog coordinates are not calibrated.
